1. The whole money concept in one picture

SmartPRS has one golden rule: money first, account after. A workspace (account) is created only after verified payment — or after Ametecs staff deliberately records an offline payment or approves credit. There is no free trial; evaluation happens in a shared passkey-protected demo that wipes itself.

Path A — Self-serve buy (primary)

Landing pricing→/signup cart
(plan+price on one page)→Razorpay pays→Workspace created + invoice PAID + receipt→Auto-login → set password

Path B — Quotation (finance team)

/signup → “Send me a Quotation”→Email: PDF + pay link→Finance pays /quote/{token}
(no login needed)→Same result as Path A

Path C — Offline / credit (admin)

Money via NEFT/UPI/cheque/cash — or credit promise→Admin → Quotations → Record payment
Paid / Partial / Due→Workspace live immediately;
balance tracked till zero

Path D — Renewal / upgrade (existing customer)

/app → My Subscription→Renew (full period) or Upgrade (pro-rata difference)→Invoice + receipt automatic

Path E — On-premises perpetual licence (admin-driven)

Admin records client + price→Email invoice + public pay link→Pay online or record offline→Full payment auto-issues the licence key / .lic file
Why it feels smooth: at every entry point the visitor sees the exact final price (including GST) before paying, pays without creating anything first, and the system builds everything automatically the second the money is verified — account, subscription, GST invoice marked PAID, receipt email with PDF, WhatsApp confirmation, and auto sign-in.

2. Front side — the public website

2.1 Landing page & pricing section

The landing page (navy + orange design, CMS-editable at /admin/landing) sells the demo first: the main hero button is “Request a Demo” (scrolls to the lead form) and the nav highlights “Live Demo” in orange.

Pricing section (“Simple, scalable plans”):

  • 3 plan cards from the CMS — Starter ₹1,000/mo (up to 25 employees), Growth ₹2,500/mo (75, marked “★ Most popular” with orange border), Professional ₹5,000/mo (150). All show “+ GST”. Bullets list the extra-employee rate (₹60/₹50/₹40 per month), extra company ₹1000/mo, and the discount ladder: minimum 3 months advance 0%, 6 months 10% off, 12 months 25% off.
  • Every card has one button — “Get started” → /signup?plan={name}. The chosen plan arrives pre-selected on the cart page.
  • No calculator on the landing page. All live pricing maths happens on /signup. Landing prices are simple CMS text.
  • Below the grid, a navy On-premise band: “Prefer to own it outright?” with 3 edition chips (Basic / Advance / Collection DNA) and a “Talk to sales →” button to the contact form. No on-prem prices are published.

2.2 Lead form (demo request) — the funnel entry

Bottom of the landing page: “Book a live demo”. Fields: Full Name*, Company*, Designation, City*, Mobile*, Official Email*, Employees (size band dropdown: 1-25 / 26-75 / 76-150 / More than 150), Challenges (free text). Hidden honeypot field blocks bots.

  • “Schedule My Demo” — validates, then opens WhatsApp with all the details pre-filled AND saves the lead via POST /lead in the background. The visitor is told “tap Send on WhatsApp to confirm”.
  • “Chat on WhatsApp” — refuses to open until the form is filled (“Please fill your details first so we receive them on WhatsApp”), then opens wa.me/919000098877 with the details.
  • Every saved lead instantly emails sales (sales@ametecsindia.com) and fires a WhatsApp alert to Ametecs — recipients configurable in the Landing CMS.
  • Lead statuses: new → contacted → closed, worked at /admin/leads. A demo-passkey request also auto-creates a lead (source “live_demo”).
Important as-is fact: leads and purchases are completely separate — there is no automatic lead→customer conversion. Sales works the lead by hand and points the prospect at /signup or raises a quotation for them.

2.3 Live demo passkey gate (/demo) — evaluation without accounts

  • Visitor enters name, WhatsApp number, email, company → a 6-digit passkey arrives by email + WhatsApp (never shown in the browser). Same page has the passkey box → enters a fully-loaded shared demo workspace with a guided tour.
  • Passkey validity: Super-Admin setting, default 2 hours (1–72 allowed). When it lapses: auto-logout and the demo wipes itself (15-min scheduler + daily 03:30 full reset).
  • Edition-specific gates exist at /app1 /app2 /app3 /teamdemo for sales staff to demonstrate L1/L2/L3/full editions.
  • Admin register (in-app → Demo Requests): sees requests, can read the PIN, resend, revoke, change validity hours.
  • Every new demo request also lands as a lead so sales follows up.

2.4 The /signup cart page — the heart of the system

One page does everything. Headline: “Start your SmartPRS workspace” — “All 16 modules in every plan · pay only for headcount · GST invoice emailed instantly”. Two columns:

Left — “Your organisation”

  • Company/group name*, Your name*, Work email* (this becomes the login), Mobile.
  • State* (36-state dropdown — “decides GST on your invoice: Telangana = CGST+SGST, other states = IGST, same total either way”), GSTIN (optional, 15 chars, printed on the tax invoice; first 2 digits must match the state).
  • Web address (optional): smartprs.com/c/______ — 3–10 letters, becomes the client’s branded login page; auto-generated from the company name if left blank.
  • Total employees (default 25) and Companies in group (default 1) — both re-price the summary live on every keystroke.

Right — “Plan & billing”

  • 3 plan tiles (from the plans DB table — price, included employees, extra-employee rate). Clicking re-prices.
  • 3 billing-cycle tiles: Quarterly (standard) · Half-yearly (10% off) · Annual (25% off). Default quarterly.
  • Coupon box (shown only if any public coupon exists) with Apply button. Plus the “exclusive-offer catch”: the moment the email field is filled, the page silently asks the server whether a personal offer was sent to that email and auto-applies it — “🎁 Your exclusive offer was applied automatically”.
  • Live price summary, always visible: plan line, extra-employee line, extra-company line (₹1,000/mo each), billing period, advance discount (green), coupon (green), Subtotal, GST 18%, and a navy “Payable now ₹X” pill.
  • Terms checkbox — opens a full Terms & Refund modal (7-day money-back guarantee, minimum 3 months billing, DPDP data terms) with an “I Accept” button that ticks the box.
  • Action 1 (solid orange): “Pay securely & create workspace” — server re-prices everything (browser numbers are never trusted), creates a pending signup record + Razorpay order, opens the Razorpay modal (UPI/card/netbanking), verifies the payment signature, then provisions everything and auto-signs the buyer in. Success pane: “Welcome aboard!” with “Open my workspace →” (auto-redirects after 2.5 s).
  • Action 2 (outline): “Send me a Quotation” — same details become a formal quotation instead: PDF emailed with a secure pay link, valid 15 days. Fine print: “Need approval from your finance team first?”. The terms checkbox is not required for a quotation.
As-is detail worth knowing: there is no OTP or email verification anywhere in signup — the payment itself is the proof of intent. OTP exists only on the demo gate.

2.5 Public quotation pay page — /quote/{token}

Token-secured, no login. Shows “Quotation PRS-Q-2026-27-08-0001 · Prepared for {company}”. The page has four automatic states:

Paying a fresh quote runs the identical provisioning as a direct signup — including auto sign-in for whoever paid.

2.6 After payment — the arrival experience

  • Auto sign-in happens in the paying browser → redirected into the app → forced to “Create password & open my workspace” (first-password screen; the emailed temporary password is only the backup).
  • Backup channels regardless: a welcome email (temporary password + 72-hour set-password link + “Your branded sign-in page: /c/{slug} — bookmark it!”) and the payment-receipt email with the GST tax-invoice PDF attached.
  • WhatsApp (Interakt, best-effort): a welcome template and a payment-confirmation template.
  • Branded login: every client gets /c/{slug} — their own login page URL.

3. Backend logic — how the money engine works

3.1 The pricing formula (single source of truth)

One function — BillingController::priceFor(plan, seats, cycle, companies) — is used by the signup page, quotations, renewals, upgrades, admin screens and PDFs. Browser numbers are never trusted; every order is re-priced server-side.

StepRule
Per-month priceplan base price + (extra employees beyond the plan’s included count × per-employee rate) + ₹1,000 × each additional company
Periodquarterly = 3 months (0% off) · half-yearly = 6 months (10% off) · annual = 12 months (25% off). Monthly exists only for legacy records.
Amountper-month × months × (1 − discount)
Couponapplied after the cycle discount (they stack), before GST
GSTflat 18% on the post-discount amount

3.2 Tables & statuses (the order model)

There is no “orders” table. The pending record that owns the Razorpay order id depends on the purchase kind:

PurchaseRecordStatus journeyRazorpay receipt
New signup, pay nowsignupspending → provisionedSIGNUP-{id}
Quotationsignupsquoted → provisionedQUOTE-{id}
Quote balance (credit)signups (already provisioned)— (balance computed)QUOTE-{id}-BAL
Renewal / upgraderenewalspending → doneRENEW-{id}
Admin invoice collectioninvoicesdue → paidinvoice number
On-prem licenceonprem_clients + onprem_paymentsderived from paid_totalLIC-{id}
Golden accounting rule: “partial” is never a stored status anywhere. Invoice statuses are only due and paid. The balance is always computed live: total − sum of the payments ledger. That is why the books can never disagree with the money.

3.3 What happens the moment payment succeeds (provisioning chain)

After Razorpay signature verification (or an admin offline entry), one function builds everything, in this order:

  • 1. Tenant record + first company + admin user created (admin user is flagged “must set own password”).
  • 2. Subscription row written as active at the full price (a coupon discounts only the first invoice — renewals bill full rate).
  • 3. Tenant’s monthly-revenue figure (MRR) updated.
  • 4. GST invoice created (due) → payment recorded against it → flips to paid.
  • 5. Coupon redemption counted — only now, after confirmed money.
  • 6. Receipt email with tax-invoice PDF; welcome email with credentials + branded login link.
  • 7. Auto sign-in for the paying browser; WhatsApp welcome + payment templates.

Everything customer-facing is fail-soft: a broken email or WhatsApp never rolls back the money or the workspace.

Offline variant (admin “Record payment”): same chain minus Razorpay and minus auto sign-in. Paid = full entry · Partial = part now, balance on credit · Due = ₹0 now, all on credit. All three provision the workspace immediately — credit is a commercial judgement; the client never waits for the last rupee. The credit due date becomes the invoice due date.

3.4 Quotations

  • A quotation is a signups row with status quoted — number series PRS-Q-{FY}-{MM}-{NNNN} (own series, resets each 1 April; quotes are not tax documents).
  • Raised from the public signup page by the prospect themselves — or by staff filling the same form on the client’s behalf. No pre-created account needed.
  • The coupon (typed or exclusive) is locked into the quote — the pay link honours it even if the code expires later.
  • Email goes out instantly: quotation PDF attached, “valid 15 days”, CTA “Pay securely & create workspace” → /quote/{token}.
  • There is no approval workflow — approval is expressed by paying. Statuses: quoted → provisioned, nothing else.
  • Validity is display-only: an expired quote’s pay link still technically accepts payment at the locked price.

3.5 Invoices, numbering & GST

  • Number format: PRS-{FY}-{MM}-{NNNN} e.g. PRS-2026-27-08-0001. Indian financial year (Apr–Mar), one consecutive series, resets 1 April. Generated as MAX(existing)+1 so deletions can never duplicate — and the series is shared between SaaS invoices and on-prem licence invoices.
  • GST split: 18% total always. Buyer GSTIN’s first 2 digits (or state name) vs seller state 36-Telangana: match → CGST 9% + SGST 9%; else IGST 18%. Seller profile (name, GSTIN 36AAHCT0971F1ZB, address, SAC 998314) env-overridable.
  • Invoice statuses: due / paid only. Auto invoices are due in 7 days.
  • Two recording functions with strict separation: full-settlement (writes the whole invoice amount, used by gateway paths) and partial-aware (writes the exact amount, settles only when the ledger covers the total ± 1 paisa — used by credit/balance paths).
  • Payments ledger: every payment row keeps gateway, method, transaction reference; idempotent per gateway transaction id (a payment can never record twice).

3.6 Coupons & exclusive offers

  • Coupon fields: code (uppercase, unique), percent or flat, value, valid-till, max uses, minimum cycle, plan restriction, once-per-customer (default on), applies to signup/renewal/both, status active/disabled, internal note.
  • Exclusive offers: a coupon locked to one email. Admin fills “Send an exclusive offer by email” → the client receives it → when that email is typed on signup/renewal/quote, the discount applies automatically with no code typing.
  • Discount maths: applied to the pre-GST amount after the cycle discount; flat capped at the amount; GST recomputed.
  • Redemption counted only after confirmed payment; every redemption logged (who, context, amount discounted).
  • The subscription row always keeps the full price — a coupon discounts only that first invoice.

3.7 Webhook safety net

POST /webhooks/razorpay (signature-verified) handles payment.captured / order.paid: matches the invoice by gateway order id (or notes), records the payment and emails the receipt — so a buyer who closed the browser mid-payment still gets everything. If no invoice matches, it checks the renewals table and completes an interrupted renewal. Unknown events return 200 (stop retries); processing errors return 500 (Razorpay retries).

4. Logged-in money screens

4.1 My Subscription (tenant admin only)

  • Plan card: plan name + status pill, employees subscribed (across all companies), included limits, employees actually in the workspace, companies subscribed vs in use (red “OVER LIMIT — upgrade on renewal” if exceeded), billing period, period amount, active-until date with days left. Alert bars when expiring ≤15 days or expired.
  • Invoices card: last 24 invoices with status and tax-invoice PDF download.
  • “Renew / Upgrade” modal: choose mode —
    • Renew/extend: full period; “renewing early never loses days” — new period starts when the current one ends. Coupon box with the same exclusive-offer auto-apply.
    • Upgrade now: pay only the pro-rata difference for the remaining days; limits increase immediately; the expiry date does not move; future renewals bill the new rate. No coupons on upgrades.
  • Strict guards: renewal must cover at least the companies and active employees currently in use (“exited employees free their seat”). Downgrades apply at renewal only.
  • Razorpay in the modal → verified → toast “Payment received — your subscription is active until {date}. The tax invoice has been emailed.”

4.2 Expiry, grace & lock behaviour

StateWhenWhat happens
activeWithin periodNormal. If ≤15 days left: navy bottom bar “expires in N days” + Renew link (admin only; employees never see money bars).
graceUp to 7 days past expiryApp fully usable; amber bottom bar “expired on {date} — workspace suspends after the grace period”.
lockedPast grace, or subscription cancelled/suspendedEmployees blocked; the admin can still sign in but every navigation is forced to My Subscription to renew. Red bar: “workspace is suspended for your team”.
noneNo subscription recordNever gated (legacy/manual tenants). Any billing-code error also fails to “none” — a billing bug must never brick a paying customer.

Renewal instantly clears an auto-suspend. Manual suspension by staff (Tenants screen) additionally disables all the tenant’s user logins.

4.3 Reminder (dunning) engine — daily 08:00

  • Email + WhatsApp reminders at 15, 7, 3, 1 and 0 days before expiry, then daily through the 7-day grace. Each has warm copy ending “Renew in a minute from Administration → My Subscription”.
  • After grace with no renewal: tenant flagged suspended (dashboard flag; the real lock is the middleware — the admin can still log in to renew).
  • Every alert is logged (kind, channel, sent/failed) and shown to the Super Admin under Subscriptions → “Expiry alert log”. Idempotent — each reminder goes once per period per channel.
  • A second daily job raises renewal invoices for subscriptions past their renewal date and emails them.

Every lead was already emailed/WhatsApped to sales the moment it arrived.

5.3 Coupons desk — /admin/coupons

“New coupon” form (code, %/flat, value, valid till, max uses, minimum cycle, applies-to, plan ticks, once-per-customer, campaign note) · “Send an exclusive offer by email” form (email, name, %/flat, value, valid days default 10 — creates the locked coupon and emails it) · coupon list with EXCLUSIVE badges, usage counts, Enable/Disable/Delete · redemption log (latest 100: when, code, context, company/email, workspace, discount given).

5.4 On-Prem licence sales desk — /admin/onprem

The perpetual-licence pipeline, per client card:

  • Record the client: company, contact, GSTIN/state, edition (L1/L2/L3), employee band, licence price, AMC % (default 18), validity (term or exact date), on-expiry behaviour (renew field vs notice only).
  • Money strip: Price (+GST total) · Paid (green when full, amber when partial) · Balance · AMC % · invoice number + public pay link. Toggle: “Allow activation on partial payment” (audited in the notes).
  • Actions: Record payment (amount + NEFT/cheque/UPI/gateway/cash + reference) · Email invoice + pay link (GST invoice PDF, shared PRS number series; public page /licence/{token} collects the balance by Razorpay) · Generate licence key — blocked until fully paid unless the partial-activation tick is on (“Key NOT generated: payment is partial (₹X of ₹Y incl. GST)…”) · Generate/re-download node-locked .lic file (fingerprint + seat cap) · Renew licence (AMC extension) · Release server binding · Revoke · Delete.
  • Auto-issue: when an online payment on the public pay page completes the full amount, the licence key is generated and emailed automatically — no staff action.

5.5 Super-admin billing screens (in-app → /app/billing)

  • Subscriptions: every tenant’s plan/seats/companies/cycle/amount/status/renewal/expiry (LOCKED red, GRACE amber, ≤15 days orange) + edit modal (can also set trialing/suspended/cancelled manually — the only place “trialing” can ever be set). Below: the Expiry alert log.
  • Invoices: generate for a tenant, PDF, email, “Mark paid (test)” (manual settlement), or drive a live Razorpay checkout while sitting with the client.
  • Payments: read-only ledger of the last 500 payments (invoice, tenant, amount, gateway, method, txn).
  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay Test/Live keys + webhook secret (encrypted at rest; blank keeps stored), with the webhook URL to paste into the Razorpay dashboard and a “Live mode not ready” warning until everything is set.
  • Tenants / Plans: tenant GST profile (state datalist + GSTIN, “printed on invoices”), suspend (blocks all logins), plans CRUD (base price, per-employee rate, included employees, default cycle, module groups; delete refused while any tenant references it).

8. Rough edges found (as-is observations — nothing was changed)

  • An apostrophe in the buyer’s name can break the pay button on the public quote page (name is printed raw into the payment script).
  • An expired quotation’s pay link still accepts payment (expiry is display-only).
  • The SaaS invoice PDF never shows Received/Balance rows — a 99%-paid invoice prints the same amber DUE as an unpaid one (only the on-prem invoice PDF shows balance).
  • On-prem manual payments have no overpayment guard, and the auto-issue email can state a different AMC date than the licence actually gets.
  • The signup page’s plan/cycle tiles stay 3-across on small phones (tight), and the first-password screen lacks the mobile-stacking fix the login page has.
  • Generating a .lic file is not payment-gated (unlike the licence key button).

9. Decision recorded for SmartEPT (Ejaz, 6 Aug 2026)

The 7-day free trial becomes its own separate path — captured as a LEAD, not mixed into buying. Buy buttons must lead to payment; the trial is a sales-nurtured evaluation request, like SmartPRS treats demo requests (every demo request lands in the Leads desk for follow-up). Detailed mapping of this SmartPRS system onto SmartEPT will be presented separately for approval before anything is built.
