⚙ Owner run-steps (Central)

  1. Migrate — migrate.bat (or php artisan migrate): creates plan_perpetual_bands.
  2. Seed the v2 pricing — php artisan db:seed --class=Database\Seeders\PricingV2Seeder. Idempotent; creates the single product + both band sets and deactivates the old plans.
  3. Clear caches — php artisan cache:clear (public plans are cached 5 min), restart PHP / clear OPcache, hard-refresh.
  4. Runtime test (do before go-live): Admin → raise a Cloud quote at a couple of scales (e.g. 100 & 500 users, annual) and a Perpetual quote — confirm totals match the tables above; then take one order through pay link → invoice and confirm the GST document. Open the public /pricing and click through the scale picker + cycle toggle.

Decisions baked in (change any of these easily)

  • Cloud numbers are the prototype figures you approved (₹45→₹6 annual). Editable per band in Central → Plans (or the seeder).
  • AMC % stored at 18% (mid of 15–20). Editable via the pricing_amc_pct setting.
  • Year-1 AMC is included on a perpetual purchase (licence sets AMC expiry +1 year); optional thereafter. Tell me if you'd rather AMC be paid from day one.
  • Setup at ₹100/user above 30 grows large at big scale (at 5,000 users it computes ~₹5 lakh). Still live as specified — say the word and I'll add a cap or a lower rate above a threshold.
  • Existing customers: old client-hosted subscriptions no longer have a matching product; their renewals would now price on the Cloud bands. We should agree a migration/grandfather rule for any live ones.

Rollback

Fully reversible: re-activate the old plans (core/professional/enterprise rows still exist) and revert the two commits. No data was deleted; no order, invoice, payment or licence was modified.
