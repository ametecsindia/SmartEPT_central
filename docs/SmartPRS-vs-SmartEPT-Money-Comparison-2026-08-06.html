SmartPRS vs SmartEPT — Money Matters, Side by Side

How each product takes a visitor from “interested” to “paid customer”, and how the money is managed afterwards — compared honestly, exactly as both exist in the live code today. With screenshots of the actual pages.

Prepared 6 August 2026 · From live trees C:\laragon\www\smartprs2 and C:\laragon\www\smartept-central · Companion to the “SmartPRS Money System — As-Is Deep Scan” document

1. The verdict in one line

SmartPRS: money first, account after — one page shows the price and takes the payment; everything else is automatic. Smooth.

SmartEPT: account first, money hidden at the end — the Buy buttons land on a trial-only page that shows a price but has no way to pay. The buyer must finish a trial signup and then hunt for the purchase inside the portal. Confusing.

Below the surface it flips: SmartEPT's accounting engine is stronger than SmartPRS (a true payments ledger, refunds with credit notes, Stripe as a second gateway, CGST/SGST snapshots on every invoice). The problem is only the front door.

4. The full buyer journey, step by step

SmartPRS — from click to working workspace: 3 steps, ~2 minutes

Pricing: “Get started”→/signup: fill one page, see ₹ total, Pay→Workspace created · invoice PAID · receipt emailed · auto-logged-in · set password

SmartEPT — from click to paid licence: 8 steps, easy to get lost

Pricing: “Buy a licence”→Trial-only signup page→Enter details + OTP email→Trial account created (wrong outcome — he wanted to BUY)→Portal → find “Plans / Buy”→Create order→Open /pay link · Razorpay→Licence + invoice + receipt (this part is automatic and solid)
The confusion in one sentence: SmartEPT shows the money but takes the visitor to a trial; SmartPRS shows the money and takes it.

6. What each product should envy in the other

What SmartEPT should take from SmartPRS
  • The one-page cart + pay front door (pay before account, auto-provision, auto-login).
  • Self-serve quotation with PDF + public pay link from the public page.
  • Admin raising quotes for prospects without pre-creating an account.
  • The expiry ladder: reminders → grace → lock-to-renew.
What SmartEPT already does better
  • The ledger door — one entry point for every rupee, refunds as negative rows with credit notes.
  • Stripe alongside Razorpay for international cards.
  • Richer GST invoices (buyer snapshot, place of supply).
  • A real 7-day trial product (once it stops swallowing the buy click) and public perpetual pricing bands.

7. Decision already recorded (Ejaz, 6 Aug 2026)

The 7-day trial becomes its own separate path, captured as a LEAD for sales follow-up — never the landing place of a Buy button. Buy buttons must lead to payment. The detailed SmartEPT rework mapping will be presented separately for approval before anything is built.
