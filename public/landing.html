Industry-First Feature

Punch at the gate.
Only then the PC works.

SmartEPT connects office attendance directly with authorised desktop access. Your biometric attendance source and SmartEPT work together so physical presence, workstation access and productive time can be reconciled in one system.

  • No gate punch → the SmartEPT agent keeps the workstation on a polite lock screen
  • Punch OUT at the gate mid-day → the PC auto-locks and an automatic break starts
  • "In the building" vs "at the desk" reported separately — see hidden gaps
  • Helps prevent buddy punching, proxy attendance and unauthorised workstation use from day one
  • Fail-safe by design: if attendance verification is unavailable, an administrator or assigned authorised person can permit login. Direct employee login may also be enabled where the administrator has explicitly granted that permission to the employee
1 · Employee punches at the gateBiometric device sends the punch to SmartEPT in real time
2 · Their PC unlocks for workSmartEPT agent verifies the gate event and allows the session
3 · Mid-day punch out? Auto-breakPC locks, break timer starts — resumes on punch-in
4 · Office-time vs desk-time reportManagement sees the gap between present and productive

Business Continuity Protected

Gate-to-PC must not unnecessarily interrupt business operations. During biometric, network or attendance-server failure, an administrator or assigned authorised person can approve employee access. Direct employee login is available only when specifically enabled by the administrator. Every fallback event should record the employee, approver, reason, date, time and access duration in the audit trail.
